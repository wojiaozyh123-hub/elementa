Elementa Hear the Elements

How it works

Light is 40 octaves above sound. Here is what the elements sound like.

光比声音高 40 个八度。把每个元素的光谱降下来，这就是它们的声音。

Light, forty octaves down

把光降低 40 个八度

Heat an element in a flame or an electric discharge and it glows at a handful of exact wavelengths — its emission spectrum, as individual as a fingerprint. Elementa takes the frequency of each of those lines and halves it forty times. That is exactly 40 octaves down, from hundreds of terahertz to hundreds of hertz:

把一种元素放进火焰或放电管里加热，它只会在少数几个精确的波长上发光——这就是它的发射光谱，像指纹一样独一无二。元素之声把每条谱线的频率连续减半 40 次，也就是整整降低 40 个八度，从几百太赫兹降到几百赫兹：

f = c / λ / 240 ≈ 272 659.7 / λnm Hz

Visible light, 380–780 nm, lands between 718 Hz and 350 Hz: every colour we can see fits in about one octave. Hydrogen’s red line at 656.5 nm becomes 415.3 Hz, a G♯. Red sounds low and violet sounds high; in stereo, red sits on the left and violet on the right — the same as on every spectrum strip here.

可见光 380–780 nm 落在 718 Hz 到 350 Hz 之间：我们看得见的所有颜色，只占大约一个八度。氢的红线 656.5 nm 变成 415.3 Hz，是一个 G♯。红色低沉、紫色高亢；在立体声里，红在左、紫在右——和这里每一条光谱条的方向一致。

Hear the beat

听见“拍”

Sodium’s famous yellow light is really two lines, 589.16 and 589.76 nm. Forty octaves down they become 462.8 and 462.3 Hz — so close that together they swell and fade about once every two seconds: a 0.5 Hz beat. Headphones help.

钠那著名的黄光其实是两条谱线：589.16 nm 和 589.76 nm。降低 40 个八度后是 462.8 Hz 和 462.3 Hz，两者靠得极近，同时响起时声音大约每两秒起伏一次——这就是 0.5 Hz 的“拍”。戴上耳机听得更清楚。

Magnified: the two D lines, 0.6 nm apart.放大后的两条 D 线，相距 0.6 nm。

Playing

玩法

  • Tap an element to hear it; drag across the table to strum.
  • Strum plays the lines from red to violet, 65 ms apart. Chord plays them all at once.
  • Keyboard: type a symbol (N, Na, Fe…) to play it. Arrow keys move, Space replays, Enter opens the details.
  • Ear Test: five mystery elements a day — the same puzzle for everyone, on the web and on iPhone.
  • Spell: type any word and hear it spelled in elements. Share it as a link or a picture.
  • 点一下元素就能听到它；在周期表上滑动可以连续弹奏。
  • 琶音从红到紫依次奏出每条谱线，间隔 65 毫秒；和弦则同时奏出。
  • 键盘：输入元素符号（N、Na、Fe……）即可演奏；方向键移动，空格重播，回车打开详情。
  • 听音测验：每天五个神秘元素——网页和 iPhone 上，所有人的题目都一样。
  • 元素拼写：输入任意英文单词，听它被元素符号拼出来，并以链接或图片分享。

The data

数据

Wavelengths and intensities come from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database — the Handbook of Basic Atomic Spectroscopic Data by J. E. Sansonetti and W. C. Martin, strong-lines tables. Air wavelengths are converted to vacuum (Ciddor), and neutral-atom lines are preferred. Each element keeps up to 8 visible lines (12 from ultraviolet to infrared). A line within 0.06–0.7 semitones of a stronger chosen line is skipped so chords stay clear, but fine-structure doublets closer than 0.06 semitones are kept, so you can hear them beat.

波长与强度来自美国国家标准与技术研究院（NIST）原子光谱数据库——J. E. Sansonetti 与 W. C. Martin 编写的 Handbook of Basic Atomic Spectroscopic Data 强谱线表。空气中的波长已换算为真空波长（Ciddor 公式），并优先采用中性原子的谱线。每种元素最多保留 8 条可见光谱线（紫外到红外共 12 条）。如果一条谱线与已选中的更强谱线相差 0.06–0.7 个半音，就跳过它，让和弦保持清晰；而相差不到 0.06 个半音的精细结构双线会保留下来，让你听见“拍”。

Some elements have only one or two strong visible lines — palladium, aluminium, chlorine — and that is the physics, not a gap. Astatine, francium and the elements from fermium onward have no lines in these tables, so they stay silent. The small dot on each tile is the element’s spectral hue: its visible lines mixed as the eye would see them, weighted by intensity.

有些元素只有一两条强可见光谱线——钯、铝、氯——这是物理事实，并非数据缺失。砹、钫以及从镄开始的元素在这些表中没有谱线，因此保持安静。每个方块角上的小圆点是该元素的光谱色：把它的可见光谱线按强度、按人眼的感知方式混合后的颜色。
